About

Music and computer science are one line of work for me, not two. I compose, I perform as a pianist and I build software, and the interesting things happen where those meet.

I studied composition at the Karol Lipiński Academy of Music in Wrocław and I am now a mathematics student. I write for chamber ensembles and orchestra, and have worked with the Wrocław Opera, Ensemble Musikfabrik and Ensemble Écoute among others. I am a prizewinner in both composition and piano competitions.

Since 2019 I have worked as a software engineer at DIVEINAI, where I am responsible for the architecture, deployment and maintenance of a production platform. My research covers medical informatics, more recently machine learning, and technology for live music: synchronising distributed performers, measuring musical tension in an audience, and various applications of the BYOD model.

Current projects

Agogica.app
A real-time synchronisation network for musical ensembles. Performers connect through their own devices and stay together through a concert: shared score navigation, measure tracking and cues that go beyond a plain click track.
IAM CoCompose 2026-2027
An Adam Mickiewicz Institute programme pairing contemporary music with Polish textile art. As a laureate of the first edition I am writing a commissioned piece for Ensemble des Équilibres.
Szkoła Pionierów PFR
A startup incubation programme run by PFR since 2018. Interdisciplinary teams take an idea through validation and prototyping to a pitch in front of investors.

Tools

These tools have a Polish interface.

Stave generator
Generates blank music staff paper: any number of staves, on any paper size, ready to print.
Golden chord
Builds chords from the golden ratio division of an interval, then approximates them in equal temperament.
Fader test
Records time series data from a slider potentiometer. Also available as a PWA. Recorded data can be reviewed on the chart page.
Receipt tracker
A simple receipt tracker. Add a photo of a receipt, let OCR read it, and it extracts the date, amount and title. Everything is stored locally in the browser.
